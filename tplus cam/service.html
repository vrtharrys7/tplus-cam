Trade-In & Buy-In Services

Upgrade your ride or sell your car to us — fair valuations, fast paperwork, zero hassle.

What We Offer

Two Simple Ways to Move On From Your Current Car

Whether you want to put your old vehicle toward your next certified pre-owned Toyota, or simply sell it for cash, Toyota Plus Cambodia makes the process transparent and effortless. Every car we take in is valued by our trained appraisers using real market data — so you always get a price you can trust.

Trade-In Service

Trade In Your Car

Bring in your current vehicle and apply its appraised value directly toward the purchase of any car in our showroom. It's the easiest way to upgrade — one visit, one transaction, no waiting for a separate sale.

  • Free, no-obligation appraisal of your vehicle
  • Trade-in value applied instantly to your new car
  • Lower the amount you finance or pay upfront
  • We handle the ownership transfer paperwork for you
  • All Toyota and non-Toyota makes accepted
Buy-In Service

Sell Your Car to Us

Not buying right now? We'll buy your car outright. Get a competitive cash offer based on a transparent inspection — settle the same day and skip the stress of finding a private buyer.

  • Fast cash offer after a quick inspection
  • Fair, market-based valuation with no lowballing
  • Same-day payment by cash or bank transfer
  • No need to advertise or meet strangers
  • We take care of de-registration and transfer
The Process

How It Works

Four straightforward steps — most appraisals are completed in a single visit.

1

Tell Us About Your Car

2

Free Inspection & Appraisal

Our trained team inspects your car and prepares a transparent, market-based valuation on the spot.

3

Get Your Offer

Choose to trade the value toward a new car, or take a cash buy-in offer — whichever suits you best.

4

We Handle the Paperwork

Sit back while we complete the ownership transfer and registration. You drive away or get paid the same day.
