What We Inspect

110+ Checkpoints, Four Categories

Blue-T evaluates a vehicle across four dimensions, documenting every finding with codes and a severity scale so the final grade is fully transparent.

Interior

Comfort and cabin condition — assessed for wear, damage and anything that affects the in-car experience.

The Process

How Blue-T Works

A fast, technology-driven process that delivers a trustworthy result in about 30 minutes.

1. Capture

The vehicle is documented and its condition recorded across all 110+ checkpoints.

2. AI Grading

The AI auto-grading algorithm analyzes every data point for precise, consistent results.

3. Detailed Report

Each finding is classified with damage codes and a clear severity scale.

4. Final Grade

You receive an overall condition grade you can trust — in around 30 minutes.

Results You Can Read

The Blue-T Grading Scale

Condition is reported on a simple letter scale — from B+ (great) down to D (needs attention).

B+

Great

Excellent condition with only minimal, hard-to-find imperfections.

B

Good

Well-kept vehicle with only light, expected wear.

B-

Average

Sound overall, with some visible cosmetic wear.

C

Okay

Usable condition with noticeable imperfections or minor repairs.

D

Need Attention

Significant wear or damage that should be reviewed carefully.
